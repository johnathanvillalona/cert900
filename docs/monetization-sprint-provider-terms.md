# Publisher and affiliate terms review

Sources reviewed **October 24, 2026 (America/New_York)**. Official public sources only. This is an application decision record, not a legal opinion or evidence of application submission. Actual account status, traffic and confirmations belong in the private application tracker. No credentials, payment details or private identity documents were accessed. Public signup and agreement text were inspected in the authorized browser.

## Decision rules

- A free inquiry or nonbinding invitation request may be submitted with verified facts under the owner's authorization. Free registration does not establish that its acceptance terms are immaterial.
- Stop before accepting exclusivity, a minimum service term, material financial/legal obligations, excessive OAuth permissions, banking/tax requirements or uncertain service-agreement acceptance.
- Application acceptance does not authorize installation, ad requests, production changes, intrusive formats or consent bypasses. Obtain explicit route/format compatibility before selecting a provider.
- Do not substitute Cloudflare requests for sessions/pageviews or treat estimated revenue as measured revenue.

## Priority advertising providers

### Infolinks

Current [www signup](https://www.infolinks.com/join-us/) was reached from the official homepage. The initially supplied sites0001 signup had a certificate error; it was not bypassed. The provider's [eligibility help](https://sites0001.infolinks.com/support/quality/what-are-the-eligibility-criteria-for-my-site-to-qualify-with-infolinks) advertises no setup fee or minimum pageviews/visitors. This is not approval or a waiver of the linked contract.

**Material acceptance gate:** the [current www Service Agreement](https://www.infolinks.com/service-agreement/) was inspected in the authorized browser: 2018, Infolinks Media LLC. It describes a 30-day trial then successive 12-month renewals, **five business days' written termination notice**, with publisher notice by mail. Revenue share and payment threshold are discretionary, not guaranteed fixed percentages/floors. Payment is 45 **working** days after month-end, subject to adjustments. Data repurposing and publisher name/logo publicity are material review points. Signup says that signing up accepts the terms. **The owner chose to pause registration and send a nonbinding inquiry; the agreement was not accepted.**

The legacy [sites0001 agreement](https://sites0001.infolinks.com/service-agreement), marked 2015, instead shows 70% for in-text, $50 and one-day notice. These conflict with the live www agreement and must **not** be quoted as the current offer. The sites0001 browser also showed a certificate error; no bypass was attempted. Formats and secure/sensitive-page restrictions need confirmation against the actual current offer. No explicit sitewide exclusivity was established; this does not make acceptance risk-free. In-text mechanics conflict with the owner's prohibition on link substitution; manual display-only support needs written confirmation.

**Application boundary:** inspect the actual checkbox and submit wording. If registering accepts that agreement, stop before acceptance; do not report the application submitted. No scripts or formats have been authorized.

### Journey by Mediavine

Official [unified application](https://publishers.mediavine.com/join). A text-fetch 404 from this research tool is not proof the browser application is unavailable.

The current [minimum requirements](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/24633185741723-Journey-Minimum-Requirements) require at least **1,000 premium sessions within 30 days**, connected GA4, original brand-safe maintained content and engagement. It lists incompatible CMS examples and excessive AI use among disqualifiers. The [August 2026 announcement](https://www.mediavine.com/blog/two-years-of-journey-by-mediavine/) identifies premium markets such as US, UK, Canada and Australia and explicitly makes **Grow optional**, replacing the older 30-day Grow requirement. Do not reinstall Grow to apply. Cert900's React/Vite/Worker architecture must be described accurately and assessed directly, not represented as WordPress.

**Material contract gate:** [Journey exclusivity](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/24656572667547-Why-Journey-requires-exclusivity) requires control of programmatic inventory; other programmatic ads.txt lines conflict. Affiliate and direct sponsored work are permitted in principle. Application-only terms must be distinguished from joining this exclusive service. Do not remove AdSense or accept exclusivity.

Public [revenue share](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23783857493787-Revenue-Share) is 70% of gross advertiser/network proceeds with no administration, hosting, setup or sales-commission fees. [Payment timing](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/29304800005275-When-Am-I-Getting-Paid) is Net65 after month-end. Current [payment thresholds](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23783790338587-Payment-Methods-and-Minimum-Thresholds) are $100 for domestic ACH/PayPal and $200 for international eCheck; wire/FX charges vary. These supersede an older local $25 claim. No payment profile is authorized here.

**UX compatibility unresolved:** the [format overview](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/25187161842715-Getting-to-know-Journey-Ads) includes in-content, adhesion, video, sidebar and overlay units. The [targeting help](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/24780355135899-Ads-are-missing-in-the-content-or-sidebar-only) says custom placements and content hints are unsupported. [Page blocking](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/25515886400539-How-to-Remove-Ads-From-Individual-Pages) is intended for individual exceptions, not most pages. **Inference:** a static-only manual unit with no learning-app SDK cannot be assumed compatible. Obtain written confirmation before activation.

The [site-sale help](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31678817117595-Selling-Your-Journey-Website-What-You-Need-to-Know) mentions a normal 30-day termination notice, but a full current service agreement/minimum term was not verified. [Termination FAQs](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/34909875253019-Site-Terminations-Frequently-Asked-Questions) allow termination for policy violations and withholding invalid-traffic revenue. Current privacy/CMP implementation and the exact GA4 OAuth scope need review at onboarding; analytics access is not approval to change analytics consent.

### Media.net

Current [publisher page](https://www.media.net/publishers) offers display, native and video solutions. The official [contact page](https://www.media.net/contact/) lists **pubsales@media.net** for publisher inquiries. A contact form is also available. Use one factual publisher inquiry if no direct application appears; this is not account creation, approval or an ad-serving agreement. The form includes marketing consent; decline optional marketing if possible. No fixed minimum traffic requirement was verified from these pages. The no-trailing-slash page's research extraction omitted the email list, so use the explicit publisher-inquiries address rather than press/support contacts.

**Material contract gate:** [Publisher Agreement](https://www.media.net/legal/) says participation accepts its terms. Net30 is stated, but no fixed revenue percentage or payout floor was located. It includes payment withholding/clawbacks, provider termination discretion, restrictive competing-keyword/similar-service clauses, non-disparagement, indemnity, and limited content/trademark licenses. Publisher privacy disclosures must explain third-party data collection. Do not treat this as unconditional non-exclusivity. No fixed minimum term or clear publisher cancellation procedure was established; obtain clarification. Do not accept the service agreement without owner approval. The different [website terms](https://www.media.net/legal/tos/) govern browsing the website and should not be substituted for publisher terms.

The [inventory policy](https://www.media.net/legal/inventorypolicies) requires supply-chain transparency and allows pausing/termination for violations. Its [privacy policy](https://www.media.net/privacy-policy/) describes tracking, partner data use and account/traffic checks. Confirm manual route/placement control, consent/CMP support, no unwanted in-text substitution and no intrusive formats before installation. These are unresolved requirements, not assurances supplied by this review.

### Carbon Ads

Official [invitation form](https://www.carbonads.net/join). The [FAQ](https://www.carbonads.net/faq), reviewed by Carbon September 2, 2026, assesses audience relevance, monthly pageviews, maintenance, capacity and policy compliance; no numerical pageview floor is stated. Targeting is English-speaking developers/designers, especially US/Canada/Europe. Cert900's IT-training audience may overlap but fit is provider judgment.

**Material acceptance gate:** joining requires exclusive Carbon ads and forbids use as backfill. A form that only requests an invitation may be permissible; inspect actual acceptance wording first. Do not accept an exclusive arrangement or displace AdSense.

FAQ payment policy: earnings recorded within 30 days and withdrawable within 60 days after month-end; $1 general floor is subject to withdrawal-method minimums ($20 PayPal, $50 check, $500 wire/$35 fee). No guaranteed CPM or published revenue share/minimum service term was established. Actual agreement governs; payout setup remains outside scope.

Review the [placement policy](https://www.carbonads.net/placement-policy) and [BuySellAds terms](https://www.buysellads.com/terms) before joining. They were discoverable but the research parser did not expose a complete reliable body; that is an unresolved acceptance step. Carbon's no-cookie marketing is not evidence of consent-free processing; pixel tracking and applicable privacy requirements still need assessment.

### Disclosure and privacy requirements

FTC [Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) requires clear, conspicuous disclosure of material relationships. Put an understandable affiliate/sponsorship disclosure near the recommendation, not only in a footer. Preserve honest editorial comparisons and free alternatives; never claim personal use or success without evidence. Provider tracking and destination-cookie requirements must be assessed separately from Cert900's analytics preference. Do not pre-load affiliate pixels, encourage consent acceptance for commissions, or weaken a learner's rejection/withdrawal controls.
